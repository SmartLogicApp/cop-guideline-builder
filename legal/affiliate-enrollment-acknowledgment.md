## Affiliate Disclosure

I acknowledge that if I recommend CMS Compliance Guardian's Service to a prospective customer and that customer purchases a qualifying subscription attributed to me, I may receive an **Affiliate Commission** from CMS Compliance Guardian LLC.

I agree to disclose that financial relationship to prospective customers when I recommend, endorse, or promote the Service.

I understand that I must not represent my recommendation as unpaid or financially disinterested when I receive an Affiliate Commission.

## CMS Inspection and Compliance Representation

I acknowledge and agree that the Service is intended to **guide and assist organizations in preparing for CMS inspections, surveys, and compliance-readiness activities.**

I understand and agree that the Service **does not guarantee a passing CMS inspection or survey.**

I will not state, imply, promise, or guarantee to any prospective customer that:

* the Service guarantees a CMS inspection or survey pass;
* the Service guarantees CMS compliance;
* the Service guarantees accreditation or certification;
* the Service guarantees licensure or payer approval;
* Company guarantees a particular regulatory outcome; or
* use of the Service guarantees that a customer will avoid deficiencies or corrective action.

I will not use phrases such as “guaranteed CMS pass,” “pass your CMS inspection,” “CMS-approved compliance,” or substantially similar language.

I understand that violating this requirement may result in immediate termination of my participation in the Affiliate Program.

## Customer Conflict Disclosure

## Commission and Payment Terms

I acknowledge that:

* the standard Affiliate Commission is **20%** of qualifying subscription revenue actually received by Company;
* payouts are made **quarterly**;
* the minimum payout amount is **$100**;
* amounts below $100 carry forward to the next quarterly payout;
* annual subscription payments do not result in an immediate payment of the entire Affiliate Commission;
* commissions attributable to an annual subscription are allocated over the subscription term and enter the quarterly payout process as they become eligible;
* refunds, chargebacks, reversals, and other legitimate adjustments may reduce or reverse commissions; and
* Affiliate Commissions already earned are not forfeited solely because my affiliate status or the Agreement is terminated.
