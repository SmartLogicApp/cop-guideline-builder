---
name: Payout statement evidence boundary
description: Limits on interpreting refund/dispute risk and transfer history as itemized financial statement entries
---

Only label a statement deduction as a refund or chargeback when an immutable amount is linked to both its source event and the payout being adjusted. Show a recorded but unattributed negative adjustment as general, never as a guessed event-specific deduction. Year-to-date paid reference totals use completed transfers to the same affiliate, not payable or merely approved commissions; the reference is not a tax filing.

**Why:** Refund and dispute risk can arrive after payment and can be partially resolved or waived; risk snapshots alone do not establish what was actually deducted from any one transfer. A fabricated line could misstate both a partner's payout and a 1099 reconciliation.

**How to apply:** For future statement detail, add an audited source-to-payout adjustment ledger with exact amounts and reconciliation before labeling event-specific lines. Preserve paid transfer history and require manual reconciliation for post-payment recovery rather than rewriting an older statement.
