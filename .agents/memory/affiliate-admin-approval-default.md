name: Affiliate admin approval default
description: Owner-directed default approval presentation and payout gate consistency.
---

Active affiliates without an administrative hold or disapproval should show Admin Approval complete by default. A pending approval flag alone is not a payout blocker; active holds, disapproval, suspension, and termination remain blockers. Keep the checklist and payout-eligibility decision consistent.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested the seven-step Get paid checklist to show Admin Approval green unless they place a hold or disapprove. Showing it green while silently requiring manual approval to become payout-eligible contradicts that instruction. This does not authorize automatic transfer or bypass the separate commission minimum, maturity period, tax, Connect, agreement, and document checks.

**How to apply:** When modifying affiliate status, payout rules, filters, or checklist projection, check the active-affiliate default and negative administrative actions together. Do not infer that a green checklist alone permits money movement.
