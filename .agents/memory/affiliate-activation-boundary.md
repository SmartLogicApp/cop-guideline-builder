---
name: Prospective affiliate activation pause
description: The boundary between pre-launch legal review and existing affiliate obligations
---

New paid affiliate activations should fail closed until reviewed program terms are explicitly enabled by the owner. This pause is prospective; it should not stop commission accrual, reversals, or settlement for partners who were already active.

**Why:** Legal review of the terms for new partners does not erase obligations already earned under existing arrangements. Blocking the ledger for active partners would create a separate payment risk.

**How to apply:** When changing affiliate approval, rate, or payout behavior, distinguish pending applicants from existing active affiliates. Preserve historical commission rows and payout flows unless the owner separately requests changes to existing obligations.
