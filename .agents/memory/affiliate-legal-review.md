---
name: Affiliate legal-review boundary
description: Boundary between public vendor/affiliate recruitment and older affiliate finance features.
---

New vendor/affiliate recruitment should collect applications for manual follow-up without promising specific commission rates, payout rules, or agreement clauses. An application is not enrollment. Keep applicant review distinct from the pre-existing affiliate finance controls.

**Why:** The owner said commercial and legal terms are still awaiting attorney review; older program code contains specific financial rules that should not become promises to new applicants.

**How to apply:** When changing public recruitment, confirmation messages, or the application review view, use neutral copy and keep new applicants pending. Treat any future activation of paid partnerships or publishing of commercial terms as a separate, owner-approved legal-review decision. Do not erase existing partner records merely to enforce the recruitment boundary.
