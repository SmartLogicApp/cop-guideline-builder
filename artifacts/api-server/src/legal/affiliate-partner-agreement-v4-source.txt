5. COMMISSION ELIGIBILITY AND COMMISSION RATE SCHEDULE

A commission is earned only on amounts actually received and retained by CMS Compliance Guardian from the applicable Qualifying Customer. The Affiliate's commission rate is determined under the Commission Rate Schedule in this Section.

The Affiliate's initial commission rate will be either:

1. Twenty percent (20%); or

2. Ten percent (10%),

as determined by an authorized CMS Compliance Guardian administrator and recorded in the Company's Affiliate Program system when the Affiliate is approved and enrolled.

The available commission rates under this Agreement are limited to:

•  Twenty percent (20%)

•  Ten percent (10%)

•  Zero percent (0%)

No other commission rate applies unless separately established by CMS Compliance Guardian in writing or through its authorized Affiliate administration system.

At the standard monthly subscription price of $299.00, a twenty-percent (20%) commission produces a commission of $59.80 on qualifying monthly subscription revenue before applicable adjustments, refunds, chargebacks, credits, taxes, reversals, or other exclusions.

The applicable commission percentage is applied to qualifying subscription revenue actually received and retained by CMS Compliance Guardian.

Affiliate does not earn commissions on:

3.	free accounts;

4.	trial accounts unless and until they become qualifying paid subscriptions;

5.	fraudulent transactions;

6.	test accounts;

7.	duplicate accounts;

8.	refunded amounts;

9.	chargebacks;

10.	payment reversals;

11.	taxes collected from customers;

12.	amounts not actually received and retained by CMS Compliance Guardian;

14.	subscriptions that do not satisfy applicable attribution requirements;

6. QUALIFYING CUSTOMERS AND QUALIFYING REFERRALS

For purposes of this Agreement, a "Qualifying Customer" means a customer or customer account that:

1.	becomes a paying customer of CMS Compliance Guardian through an authorized referral method associated with Affiliate;

2.	is properly attributed to Affiliate in CMS Compliance Guardian's systems;

3.	generates an amount on which a commission is actually payable to Affiliate; and

4.	satisfies all other applicable requirements of this Agreement.

A "Qualifying Referral" means a referral that results in a Qualifying Customer and constitutes the first time that particular customer or customer account has generated a commission for that Affiliate.

A customer does not become a new Qualifying Referral merely because the customer:

•  cancels;

•  changes subscription status;

•  changes payment information;

•  creates a substantially duplicate account;

•  uses a different referral link;

•  changes contact information; or

•  later resubscribes under circumstances that, in substance, represent the same customer relationship.

CMS Compliance Guardian may reasonably determine whether a referral constitutes a Qualifying Referral based on its customer, billing, subscription, referral, attribution, and account records.

A Qualifying Referral must represent a genuine new customer relationship and may not be manufactured solely to satisfy Affiliate's activity requirement or obtain or preserve a higher commission rate.



7. COMMISSION RATE ACTIVITY REQUIREMENT

To maintain an ongoing commission rate, Affiliate must generate at least one new Qualifying Referral during each applicable activity period.

For purposes of this Agreement, Affiliate's current activity period is anchored to the later of:

•  the date of Affiliate's most recent new Qualifying Referral; or

•  the effective date of Affiliate's then-current commission rate.

Each activity period consists of twelve (12) months.

Following expiration of the applicable twelve-month activity period, Affiliate receives an additional sixty (60) calendar-day grace period during which Affiliate may generate a new Qualifying Referral without a reduction in the then-current commission rate.

A new Qualifying Referral during the applicable activity period or sixty-day grace period satisfies the activity requirement and resets the applicable activity-period clock as provided in this Agreement.

A customer returning after cancellation or inactivity does not constitute a new Qualifying Referral merely because the customer resumes paying for the Service.



8. REDUCTION FROM TWENTY PERCENT TO TEN PERCENT

If Affiliate is receiving a twenty-percent (20%) commission rate and does not generate a new Qualifying Referral during the applicable twelve-month activity period or during the subsequent sixty-day grace period, Affiliate's commission rate will decrease prospectively from:

20% → 10%

The reduction becomes effective upon expiration of the applicable sixty-day grace period.

Upon the effective date of the ten-percent (10%) commission rate, a new twelve-month activity period begins for purposes of determining whether Affiliate maintains or changes its ten-percent (10%) rate.

The reduction from twenty percent (20%) to ten percent (10%) does not, by itself, eliminate commissions on properly attributed existing customers.

Existing customer commissions remain subject to all other requirements, exclusions, customer attribution rules, payment requirements, and eligibility requirements of this Agreement.



9. REDUCTION FROM TEN PERCENT TO ZERO PERCENT

If Affiliate is receiving a ten-percent (10%) commission rate and does not generate a new Qualifying Referral during the applicable twelve-month activity period or during the subsequent sixty-day grace period, Affiliate's commission rate will decrease prospectively from:

10% → 0%

The reduction becomes effective upon expiration of the applicable sixty-day grace period.

Upon reaching a zero-percent (0%) commission rate, Affiliate will not earn commissions on new subscription payments unless and until Affiliate's commission rate is restored under this Agreement or CMS Compliance Guardian separately approves a different commission rate in writing.

The reduction to zero percent does not retroactively eliminate commissions that were properly earned and payable before the effective date of the zero-percent rate.



10. RESTORATION TO TWENTY PERCENT

If Affiliate's commission rate is ten percent (10%), a new Qualifying Referral will immediately restore Affiliate's commission rate to twenty percent (20%), provided Affiliate otherwise remains eligible under this Agreement.

Upon restoration to twenty percent (20%), a new twelve-month activity period begins measured from the date of the new Qualifying Referral, followed by the applicable sixty-day grace period.

If Affiliate's commission rate is zero percent (0%), a new Qualifying Referral generated within sixty (60) calendar days after the effective date of the zero-percent rate will restore Affiliate's commission rate to twenty percent (20%).

Upon restoration from zero percent to twenty percent, a new twelve-month activity period begins measured from the date of the Qualifying Referral.

The automatic restoration opportunity from zero percent to twenty percent expires at the end of the sixty-day restoration period.

After expiration of that sixty-day period, restoration from a zero-percent rate is not automatic and requires separate approval by CMS Compliance Guardian.

CMS Compliance Guardian may approve, deny, or condition any such restoration in its reasonable discretion.

3.	properly earned commissions otherwise payable before the customer's inactivity remain subject to this Agreement.

If the customer returns within sixty (60) calendar days after cancellation or the applicable inactive date, the original Affiliate may resume receiving commissions on the customer's actual paid subscription if:

1.	the customer remains properly attributed to the original Affiliate;

2.	the original Affiliate remains eligible under this Agreement; and

3.	the customer has not been properly reassigned to another Affiliate.

No commission is payable for any period during which the customer is not actually paying CMS Compliance Guardian.

A returning customer does not constitute a new Qualifying Referral for purposes of satisfying Affiliate's activity requirement when that customer previously generated a commission for that Affiliate.

If the customer leaves CMS Compliance Guardian and is subsequently brought back through a new Qualifying Referral from a different Affiliate, the new Affiliate may receive commissions going forward after the new attribution becomes effective.

The former Affiliate will not continue receiving commissions for the same customer subscription period after the new Affiliate's attribution becomes effective.
